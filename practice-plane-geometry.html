Key Concepts

  • Pythagorean Theorem — a² + b² = c². Common triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17
  • 30-60-90 Triangle — Sides are x, x√3, 2x (opposite 30°, 60°, 90°)
  • 45-45-90 Triangle — Sides are x, x, x√2
  • Circle Formulas — Area = πr². Circumference = 2πr. Arc length = (θ/360)×2πr. Sector area = (θ/360)×πr²
  • Supplementary Angles — Sum to 180°. Complementary — Sum to 90°
  • Triangle Angles — Always sum to 180°. Exterior angle = sum of two non-adjacent interior angles
  • Volume — Rectangular box: lwh. Cylinder: πr²h. Surface area of cylinder: 2πr² + 2πrh
  • Similar Figures — If sides are in ratio k, areas are in ratio k², volumes in ratio k³

1. In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite the 30° angle is 7. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

  • A. 7√2
  • B. 7√3
  • C. 14
  • D. 14√3
  • E. 21
C. In a 30-60-90 triangle, the hypotenuse is twice the short leg: 2 × 7 = 14.

2. What is the arc length of a 90° arc of a circle with radius 6?

  • F. 3π
  • G. 6π
  • H. 9π
  • J. 12π
  • K. 36π
F. Arc length = (90/360) × 2π(6) = (1/4)(12π) = 3π.

3. Two angles are supplementary. One angle is 40° more than the other. What is the larger angle?

  • A. 70°
  • B. 90°
  • C. 100°
  • D. 110°
  • E. 140°
D. x + (x + 40) = 180. 2x = 140, x = 70. Larger angle = 110°.

4. In a 45-45-90 triangle, each leg has length 8. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

  • F. 4√2
  • G. 8
  • H. 8√2
  • J. 16
  • K. 8√3
H. In a 45-45-90, hypotenuse = leg × √2 = 8√2.

5. What is the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides of length 10 and 14, and a height of 6?

  • A. 48
  • B. 60
  • C. 72
  • D. 84
  • E. 144
C. Area = ½(b1 + b2)h = ½(10 + 14)(6) = ½(24)(6) = 72.

6. A cylinder has a radius of 3 and a height of 10. What is its surface area?

  • F. 30π
  • G. 60π
  • H. 78π
  • J. 90π
  • K. 108π
H. SA = 2πr² + 2πrh = 2π(9) + 2π(3)(10) = 18π + 60π = 78π.

7. An inscribed angle in a circle intercepts an arc of 120°. What is the measure of the inscribed angle?

  • A. 30°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 120°
  • E. 240°
B. An inscribed angle is half the intercepted arc: 120° ÷ 2 = 60°.

8. In a parallelogram, one angle measures 65°. What is the measure of the adjacent angle?

  • F. 65°
  • G. 90°
  • H. 115°
  • J. 130°
  • K. 180°
H. Adjacent angles in a parallelogram are supplementary: 180° − 65° = 115°.
