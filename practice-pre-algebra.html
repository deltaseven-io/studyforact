Key Concepts

  • PEMDAS — Parentheses, Exponents, Multiply/Divide (left to right), Add/Subtract (left to right)
  • Percents — To convert decimal to percent, multiply by 100. Percent of a number: (percent/100) × number
  • Mean (Average) — Sum of all values ÷ number of values. To find a missing value, use: missing = (desired mean × count) − sum of known values
  • Ratios & Proportions — If ratio is a:b and total is T, then part a = (a/(a+b)) × T
  • GCF & LCM — GCF uses the lowest powers of shared primes. LCM uses the highest powers of all primes
  • Absolute Value — |x| is the distance from 0. If |x − a| = b, then x = a + b or x = a − b
  • Scientific Notation — a × 10n where 1 ≤ a < 10. Move decimal n places right if n is positive

1. What is the value of 2(3 + 4)² ÷ 7?

  • A. 7
  • B. 10
  • C. 14
  • D. 49
  • E. 98
C. Parentheses first: 3 + 4 = 7. Exponent: 7² = 49. Multiply: 2 × 49 = 98. Divide: 98 ÷ 7 = 14.

2. Express 0.045 as a percent.

  • F. 0.045%
  • G. 0.45%
  • H. 4.5%
  • J. 45%
  • K. 450%
H. Multiply by 100: 0.045 × 100 = 4.5%.

3. A student scored 72, 85, 91, and 68 on four tests. What score does she need on the fifth test to have an average of 80?

  • A. 78
  • B. 80
  • C. 82
  • D. 84
  • E. 86
D. Needed sum: 5 × 80 = 400. Current sum: 72 + 85 + 91 + 68 = 316. Fifth score: 400 − 316 = 84.

4. If 3/8 of a number is 27, what is the number?

  • F. 36
  • G. 48
  • H. 64
  • J. 72
  • K. 81
J. (3/8)n = 27. Multiply both sides by 8/3: n = 27 × 8/3 = 72.

5. What is the greatest common factor of 36 and 48?

  • A. 4
  • B. 6
  • C. 8
  • D. 12
  • E. 24
D. 36 = 2² × 3² and 48 = 2³ × 3. GCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

6. A jacket costs $120. If the price increases by 15%, what is the new price?

  • F. $135
  • G. $138
  • H. $140
  • J. $145
  • K. $150
G. 15% of $120 = $18. New price: $120 + $18 = $138.

7. What is the median of the set {3, 7, 2, 9, 5, 1, 8}?

  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 6
  • E. 7
C. Sort the set: 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9. The middle value (4th of 7) is 5.

8. Which of the following is equivalent to 3.2 × 104?

  • F. 320
  • G. 3,200
  • H. 32,000
  • J. 320,000
  • K. 3,200,000
H. Move the decimal 4 places right: 3.2 → 32,000.
