Key Concepts

  • SOH-CAH-TOA — sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent
  • Reciprocal Functions — csc = 1/sin, sec = 1/cos, cot = 1/tan
  • Unit Circle Values — sin 30° = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, sin 45° = cos 45° = √2/2, sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2
  • Radian Conversion — degrees × π/180 = radians. Radians × 180/π = degrees
  • Pythagorean Identity — sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
  • Graphing — y = A sin(Bx + C) + D. Amplitude = |A|, Period = 2π/B, Phase shift = −C/B, Vertical shift = D
  • Law of Sines — a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C. Law of Cosines — c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C

1. In a right triangle, the adjacent side to angle θ is 8 and the hypotenuse is 17. What is cos θ?

  • A. 8/17
  • B. 15/17
  • C. 8/15
  • D. 17/8
  • E. 17/15
A. cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse = 8/17.

2. What is the value of sin(150°)?

  • F. −1/2
  • G. −√3/2
  • H. 1/2
  • J. √3/2
  • K. 1
H. 150° is in Quadrant II where sine is positive. sin(150°) = sin(180° − 30°) = sin(30°) = 1/2.

3. What is the value of tan(45°)?

  • A. 0
  • B. 1/2
  • C. √2/2
  • D. 1
  • E. √2
D. tan(45°) = sin(45°)/cos(45°) = (√2/2)/(√2/2) = 1.

4. What is the amplitude of y = 3 sin(4x) + 1?

  • F. 1
  • G. 3
  • H. 4
  • J. 7
  • K. 12
G. For y = A sin(Bx) + D, amplitude = |A| = |3| = 3.

5. Convert 210° to radians.

  • A. 5π/6
  • B. 7π/6
  • C. 5π/4
  • D. 7π/4
  • E. 11π/6
B. 210 × π/180 = 210π/180 = 7π/6.

6. If sin θ = 5/13 and θ is in Quadrant I, what is tan θ?

  • F. 5/12
  • G. 12/5
  • H. 5/13
  • J. 12/13
  • K. 13/5
F. From the 5-12-13 right triangle, cos θ = 12/13. So tan θ = sin/cos = (5/13)/(12/13) = 5/12.

7. In a triangle, sides a = 5, b = 7, and the included angle C = 60°. Using the Law of Cosines, what is c²?

  • A. 24
  • B. 39
  • C. 49
  • D. 74
  • E. 109
B. c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C = 25 + 49 − 2(5)(7)(1/2) = 74 − 35 = 39.

8. From the top of a 50-foot building, the angle of depression to a point on the ground is 30°. How far is the point from the base of the building?

  • F. 25
  • G. 25√3
  • H. 50
  • J. 50√3
  • K. 100
J. tan(30°) = 50/d. Since tan(30°) = 1/√3: d = 50√3.
