Key Concepts

  • Slope — m = (y2 − y1)/(x2 − x1). Parallel lines have equal slopes. Perpendicular slopes multiply to −1
  • Point-Slope Form — y − y1 = m(x − x1). Use when you have a point and a slope
  • Distance Formula — d = √((x2−x1)² + (y2−y1)²)
  • Midpoint Formula — M = ((x1+x2)/2, (y1+y2)/2)
  • Circle Equation — (x−h)² + (y−k)² = r². Center (h,k), radius r. Complete the square if needed
  • Intercepts — x-intercept: set y = 0 and solve. y-intercept: set x = 0 and solve
  • Reflections — Across x-axis: (x, y) → (x, −y). Across y-axis: (x, y) → (−x, y)

1. What is the equation of the line passing through (1, 4) with slope 3?

  • A. y = 3x + 1
  • B. y = 3x − 1
  • C. y = 3x + 4
  • D. y = x + 3
  • E. y = 4x + 1
A. y − 4 = 3(x − 1). y = 3x − 3 + 4 = 3x + 1.

2. What is the center of the circle x² + y² − 6x + 4y = 12?

  • F. (3, 2)
  • G. (−3, 2)
  • H. (3, −2)
  • J. (−3, −2)
  • K. (6, −4)
H. Complete the square: (x²−6x+9) + (y²+4y+4) = 25. (x−3)² + (y+2)² = 25. Center = (3, −2).

3. A line passes through (0, 5) and (3, −1). What is the x-intercept?

  • A. 2
  • B. 5/2
  • C. 3
  • D. 5
  • E. −5/2
B. Slope = (−1−5)/(3−0) = −2. Equation: y = −2x + 5. Set y = 0: 2x = 5, x = 5/2.

4. What is the midpoint of the segment from (2, 3) to (8, 11)?

  • F. (4, 6)
  • G. (5, 7)
  • H. (6, 8)
  • J. (3, 4)
  • K. (10, 14)
G. Midpoint = ((2+8)/2, (3+11)/2) = (5, 7).

5. What is the area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (6, 0), and (0, 8)?

  • A. 14
  • B. 24
  • C. 28
  • D. 48
  • E. 96
B. Base = 6 along the x-axis, height = 8 along the y-axis. Area = ½ × 6 × 8 = 24.

6. Line L1 has equation 2x + 3y = 6. What is the slope of a line parallel to L1?

  • F. 2/3
  • G. −2/3
  • H. 3/2
  • J. −3/2
  • K. 2
G. Rewrite: 3y = −2x + 6, y = −(2/3)x + 2. Slope = −2/3. Parallel lines share the same slope.

7. What is the equation of the line perpendicular to y = (1/2)x + 3 that passes through (4, 1)?

  • A. y = −2x + 9
  • B. y = 2x − 7
  • C. y = −2x + 7
  • D. y = −(1/2)x + 3
  • E. y = 2x + 9
A. Perpendicular slope = −2. y − 1 = −2(x − 4). y = −2x + 9.

8. If a point (3, −4) is reflected across the x-axis, what are its new coordinates?

  • F. (−3, −4)
  • G. (−3, 4)
  • H. (3, 4)
  • J. (−4, 3)
  • K. (4, −3)
H. Reflecting across the x-axis changes the sign of y: (3, −4) → (3, 4).
