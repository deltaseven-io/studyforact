Key Concepts

  • Vertex Form — y = a(x − h)² + k. Vertex is (h, k)
  • Simplifying Rational Expressions — Factor numerator and denominator, then cancel common factors
  • Function Composition — f(g(x)): evaluate the inner function first, then plug into the outer
  • Exponential Growth/Decay — A = A0 × rt where r is the growth factor per period
  • Domain Restrictions — Denominators cannot equal 0. Expressions under even roots must be ≥ 0
  • Geometric Sequences — an = a1 × rn−1. Common ratio r = a2/a1
  • Complex Numbers — i = √(−1). i² = −1. Multiply using FOIL and replace i² with −1

1. What is the vertex of the parabola y = (x − 3)² + 5?

  • A. (3, 5)
  • B. (−3, 5)
  • C. (3, −5)
  • D. (−3, −5)
  • E. (5, 3)
A. In vertex form y = (x − h)² + k, the vertex is (h, k) = (3, 5).

2. Simplify: (x² − 9)/(x + 3)

  • F. x − 3
  • G. x + 3
  • H. x² − 3
  • J. x − 9
  • K. x + 9
F. Factor the numerator: (x − 3)(x + 3)/(x + 3) = x − 3.

3. If f(x) = x² + 1 and g(x) = 2x − 3, what is f(g(2))?

  • A. 0
  • B. 2
  • C. 5
  • D. 8
  • E. 10
B. g(2) = 2(2) − 3 = 1. Then f(1) = 1² + 1 = 2.

4. A population doubles every 5 years. If it starts at 1,000, what is the population after 15 years?

  • F. 3,000
  • G. 4,000
  • H. 6,000
  • J. 8,000
  • K. 16,000
J. 15 years = 3 doubling periods. 1,000 × 2³ = 1,000 × 8 = 8,000.

5. For what value(s) of x is the expression (x + 2)/(x² − 4) undefined?

  • A. x = 2 only
  • B. x = −2 only
  • C. x = 2 and x = −2
  • D. x = 4 only
  • E. x = −4 only
C. x² − 4 = (x − 2)(x + 2) = 0 when x = 2 or x = −2.

6. What is the 10th term of the geometric sequence 2, 6, 18, 54, …?

  • F. 1,458
  • G. 4,374
  • H. 13,122
  • J. 39,366
  • K. 118,098
J. a1 = 2, r = 3. a10 = 2 × 39 = 2 × 19,683 = 39,366.

7. Solve: √(2x + 5) = 7

  • A. 16
  • B. 18
  • C. 20
  • D. 22
  • E. 24
D. Square both sides: 2x + 5 = 49. 2x = 44. x = 22.

8. What is (3 + 2i)(1 − i)?

  • F. 1 − i
  • G. 5 + i
  • H. 5 − i
  • J. 1 + i
  • K. 3 − 2i
H. FOIL: 3 − 3i + 2i − 2i² = 3 − i + 2 = 5 − i (since i² = −1).
