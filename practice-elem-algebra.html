Key Concepts

  • Solving Linear Equations — Distribute, combine like terms, isolate the variable. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other
  • Exponent Rules — xa × xb = xa+b. (xa)b = xab. x−n = 1/xn
  • Factoring Trinomials — x² + bx + c = (x + m)(x + n) where m × n = c and m + n = b
  • FOIL — (a + b)(c + d) = ac + ad + bc + bd
  • Simplifying Radicals — √(ab) = √a × √b. Factor out perfect squares: √75 = √25 × √3 = 5√3
  • Direct Variation — y = kx. Find k from a known pair, then use it for the unknown
  • Word Problems — Translate: "a number tripled" = 3x, "decreased by 8" = − 8, "gives" = equals

1. Solve for x: 4(x + 2) = 2(3x − 1)

  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 6
  • E. 7
C. 4x + 8 = 6x − 2. Subtract 4x: 8 = 2x − 2. Add 2: 10 = 2x. x = 5.

2. Which of the following is equivalent to x−3?

  • F. −3x
  • G. −x³
  • H. 1/x³
  • J. x/3
  • K. 3/x
H. A negative exponent means reciprocal: x−3 = 1/x³.

3. Factor completely: x² + 7x + 12

  • A. (x + 2)(x + 6)
  • B. (x + 1)(x + 12)
  • C. (x + 3)(x + 4)
  • D. (x − 3)(x − 4)
  • E. (x + 2)(x + 5)
C. Find two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7: 3 and 4. So (x + 3)(x + 4).

4. If y varies directly as x, and y = 12 when x = 4, what is y when x = 7?

  • F. 15
  • G. 18
  • H. 21
  • J. 24
  • K. 28
H. y = kx. 12 = 4k, so k = 3. When x = 7: y = 3(7) = 21.

5. Simplify: √75

  • A. 3√5
  • B. 5√3
  • C. 15
  • D. 25√3
  • E. √75
B. √75 = √(25 × 3) = √25 × √3 = 5√3.

6. What is the product of (2x + 3)(x − 5)?

  • F. 2x² − 7x − 15
  • G. 2x² + 7x − 15
  • H. 2x² − 7x + 15
  • J. 2x² − 2x − 15
  • K. 2x² + 13x − 15
F. FOIL: 2x² − 10x + 3x − 15 = 2x² − 7x − 15.

7. The sum of three consecutive integers is 87. What is the largest of the three?

  • A. 28
  • B. 29
  • C. 30
  • D. 31
  • E. 32
C. x + (x+1) + (x+2) = 87. 3x + 3 = 87, x = 28. Largest = 30.

8. If 32x = 81, what is x?

  • F. 1
  • G. 2
  • H. 3
  • J. 4
  • K. 9
G. 81 = 34, so 2x = 4 and x = 2.
